Guide

How to use HireFlow

  1. Stay logged in

    Open LinkedIn, Gmail, and ChatGPT or Gemini in Chrome and sign in as you normally do. HireFlow never asks for those passwords.

  2. Upload your resume here

    PDF only. We extract the text on this page and keep it in chrome.storage.local.

  3. On a job post, select the text or click "Apply"

    Include the hiring email if the post has one. Click the injected Apply button on LinkedIn posts or select text → right-click → HireFlow: draft & send application. Shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+Y (Mac: ⌘⇧Y).

  4. Let the AI tab work

    HireFlow talks to the ChatGPT or Gemini tab you already have open (or opens one in the background). You stay on LinkedIn.

  5. Review, then Gmail sends

    A review window shows the draft with an auto-send countdown. Pause, edit, send now, or keep a Gmail draft. After the countdown HireFlow opens Gmail compose and clicks Send.

If something breaks

ChatGPT, Gemini, and Gmail change their pages. If HireFlow cannot find the composer, refresh that tab, make sure you are logged in, and hit Retry. You can switch AI from the review window. If Send is not clicked, the compose window stays open so nothing is lost.
